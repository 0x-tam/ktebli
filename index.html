RIVERMARK / ENERGY ASSESSMENTPROPOSAL

A useful plan starts
with the right questions.

Assess three public buildings. Identify practical improvements. Give the client a clear basis for deciding what to do next.

Understand the current position

We propose reviewing twelve months of energy records alongside a site survey for each building. Conversations with facilities teams will help explain how the spaces are used and where operational constraints affect the options.

Compare realistic options

Each recommendation will identify the work involved, an indicative cost range and any further investigation needed. Savings estimates will state their assumptions; they will not be presented as guaranteed results.

Leave the client with a decision

The final report will group recommendations into immediate operational changes and longer-term works, with a proposed sequence for considering them.

THE PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Do I need a membership to order a proposal?

No. Bring a brief you already found and choose a one-off package. A free account can browse the opportunity board now. Membership checkout and automatic matching are not open yet; coverage is limited, so always check the publisher’s original notice.

What kinds of proposals can I prepare?

Grant applications, government tenders, procurement and RFP responses, and business or service proposals. Your brief determines the requirements. Ktebli prepares the written proposal using the technical inputs you supply; engineering designs, calculations and quantity surveying are not included. Official forms, professional drawings, signatures, certificates and bid securities must come from you where required.

What do I need to bring?

Start with the opportunity link or brief, your name and email. You can apply as an individual or an organisation. After payment, confirm the proposal type and currency and describe your proposed work. Team, experience, location and governance details are optional unless the opportunity itself requires them.

What if I need changes?

Request changes from your order page. Draft includes one round, Competitive includes three, and Full includes ten within two weeks. We check revised work before delivery.

Will it invent facts to strengthen my case?

The workflow checks claims against your supplied evidence and your organisation’s own sources. Missing certifications, capabilities or commitments cannot be invented to meet the brief. Unresolved factual or compliance issues hold delivery.

Does the quality score mean I will win?

No. The score measures quality against your purchased tier: at least 70/100 for Draft, 80/100 for Competitive and 90/100 for Full. It is not a probability of success or a certification of eligibility. The issuer or client makes the decision.

When will I receive my documents?

Within 4 hours of payment and receiving your essential details. We aim to deliver sooner, with every package checked before it reaches you. Track progress on your order page. Missing evidence, provider outages or a failed check can pause delivery; deadlines do not override the quality bar. A hold does not trigger an automatic refund.

How do payment and submission work?

Pay once through Stripe after the intake checks. Download your editable documents when they clear delivery checks, review them, add required official attachments and submit them yourself. Ktebli does not sign documents or submit applications on your behalf.
