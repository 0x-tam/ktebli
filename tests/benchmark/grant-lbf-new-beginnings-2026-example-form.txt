Grant Application Example Form
This form is for guidance only and not for

To apply please visit lloydsbankfoundation.org.uk
If you are using AI to help write
your application
Please make sure that you only use AI for preparing a
first draft, formatting, and spell checking.

We understand that AI tools can help charities save
time on writing applications and we support charities
using these technologies. However, we want to help
charities use this technology as best as possible.

We are seeing an increase in AI-generated applications,
and in many cases, this is not giving charities the
best chance of success. These applications are often
generic, do not give a clear voice to the organisation
and do not highlight the unique strengths of the
organisation.

We want to hear your unique voice, so please use
your time to tailor your responses to add context and
details specific to your charity and to convey your
organisation’s story or approach.

We read every single application we receive and do not
use AI in any part of our decision making.


Programme overview
This application is for a four-year, unrestricted grant of
up to £200,000. If your application is successful, the
grant will be paid in four equal, annual instalments.

There are two grant sizes available:
● £100,000 paid over four years through annual
  payments of £25,000

● £200,000 paid over four years through annual
  payments of £50,000

If your most recent annual income is less than
£100,000, you will only be eligible for a £100,000
grant.

This funding can be used to support any costs which
further your organisation’s purpose, including building
reserves and meeting core costs.


Closing date
We will close for applications at 5pm, 9 September
2026.


Additional information
This application form will help us understand if your
organisation fits the criteria and priorities for this
programme. Alongside your grant application, we will
also read your accounts and public benefit statement
on the Charity Commission or Companies House
website as well as your website or social media
channels.

As this is a new programme, application success rates
from previous rounds are not yet available, but we
anticipate application numbers will be high. Across our
programmes in 2025, we made 205 grants with a total
value of £15,670,807 and received 985 applications.

Applicants will be informed of the final outcome of their
application by 18 December 2026 at the latest.
