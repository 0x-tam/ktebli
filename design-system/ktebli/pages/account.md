# Account surfaces

- Keep account landing and sign-in typography aligned with the public site: Fraunces headings, DM Sans controls and body text, and the shared KTEBLI! wordmark.
- The account home should tell users they can create a free account and browse public opportunities. State membership availability plainly; do not imply checkout is open.
- On desktop, the sign-in page may pair concise context with a clear form card. On phones, hide secondary marketing copy and lead with the form.
- The dashboard should place the board near the top of the page. Keep the membership strip visually calm and compact so it does not overpower search and filters.
- Opportunity rows remain stacked and compact. Keep status, deadline/geography, the proposal CTA, original source, save action, and expandable details legible at every breakpoint.
