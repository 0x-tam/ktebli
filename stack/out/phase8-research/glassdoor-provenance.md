## Data-quality flags (recorded faithfully, NOT reconciled)

- **Two of the org's own pages give different 2024-2025 headline figures.** The homepage carousel
  states "1497 individuals supported during 2024-2025 / 251 guests moved off the streets and into
  housing / 337 provided with emergency shelter" (https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/). The dedicated
  `/our-impact` page states "2,524 individuals received support / 423 moved into housing / 569
  found a safe place to sleep at our night shelters" for the same 2024-2025 period. I recorded the
  **`/our-impact` figures** in `results[]` (dedicated, granular, internally consistent) and flag
  the homepage variance here rather than merging or averaging.
- **2025-26 headline count differs by one between pages.** The reports hub
  (https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/reports) says 2,382 guests / 2,258 casework; the news announcement
  (.../News/announcing-our-annual-impact-report-25-26) says 2,381 / 2,257 and contains an obvious
  date typo ("Between 1 June 2025 and 31 May 2025"). I recorded 2,382 / 2,258 from the reports hub
  and note the ±1 discrepancy.
- **20-year Review** gives cumulative-since-1999 figures ("about 5,000" sheltered, "more than 1,000"
  into accommodation, "almost 700" into work). These are approximate lifetime totals, not annual —
  deliberately NOT placed in `results[]` to avoid being read as period figures.
- **"Lucy Abraham" appears as CEO in a 2020 awards mention** on `/whatwedo`; the **current** CEO on
  the live homepage is **Jo Carter**. Only Jo Carter is recorded as current CEO.
- Charity Commission and glassdoor.org.uk both return HTTP 403 to plain WebFetch; all their pages
  here were read through a real browser session instead. Companies House was readable via WebFetch.

## What the applicant would realistically have to supply themselves (not public)

These are internal/administrative facts that are not published on any page I could fetch. They are
left blank/null in the JSON and must come from the applicant:

- **Safeguarding policy** — existence, date, and content (running night shelters they will hold one,
  but no published copy was found → `safeguarding_policy: null`).
- **Safeguarding lead** — name, role and training/date (`safeguarding_lead_*` blank).
- **Bank account in the charity's own name** — `bank_account_own_name: null`.
- **Public liability insurance** — insurer, policy number, cover level (`public_liability_insurance: null`).
- **No conflicting grant** — whether the same costs are funded by another grant; applicant-specific
  (`no_conflicting_grant: null`).
- **Lived-experience governance** — whether people with lived experience of homelessness sit on the
  board or an advisory panel; not stated on the fetched pages (`lived_experience_governance: ""`).
- **Named shelter/day-centre venues and the full partner list** — the site names only Ace of Clubs;
  the seven shelter venues and the four day centres are described but not individually named.
- **Specific project budget, delivery dates and named delivery staff** for the grant being applied
  for — none of this is public.
